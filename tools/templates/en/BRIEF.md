# Project brief

The main page about **what we are making and why**. The Producer fills it in at the start (asking you one question at a time) and
rewrites it as things get decided or change. Everything written here is your word; agents' guesses are marked "(assumption)"
until you confirm them.

_Status: not filled in. Updated: —_

## 1. In one line
What it is and who it is for.

## 2. Goal
Why the project exists. What should happen when it is done (enquiries, sales, impression, an internal tool…).

## 3. Audience
Who will use or look at it. What matters to them.

## 4. What must be there
A list of pages, features, sections. The order is the priority.

## 5. How it should look and feel
Style, mood, colours, animations, examples you like (links), and what you definitely do NOT like.

## 6. Constraints
Deadline, budget, technology (required or forbidden), hosting, domain, languages, legal.

## 7. Done when…
Checkable signs of completion: "opens on a phone", "the contact form sends an email", "loads in < 2 s".

## 8. Materials
Texts, logos, photos, access. Where they live (not the passwords themselves — keys only in `~/.config/producer/keys.env`).

## 9. Decisions along the way
Date · what was decided · in your words. (Details: `agents/registers/OPEN.md`.)

## 10. Open questions
What is still undecided and blocks work. (They also go to the blitz.)

## 11. Product compass
This is strategy, not a work queue: the queue lives only in `agents/registers/TODO.md`. Update it when something strategic is
decided, not after every delivery. If it grows to a page, move it to a separate VISION page in the work folder and leave a link here.
- **How it differs from the others:** one or two sentences.
- **What we do NOT do (and will not):** the product's borders, so agents do not pull it sideways.
- **How we will know it worked:** 2–3 measurable signs of success (not "looks nice" but "more than N enquiries a month").
- **Where next:** the next big step after launch (one line; further ideas go to the far drawer).
